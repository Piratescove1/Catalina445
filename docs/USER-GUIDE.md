# Catalina 445 App — User Guide

A complete guide to running your boat's inventory, voyages, maintenance,
provisioning, and abandon-ship checklist — on deck or offshore.

---

## Getting started

### Installing
Open the app link in your browser. For the best experience, tap **Share → Add to
Home Screen** so it launches full-screen like a normal app.

### Your account
The first time you open the app you'll **create an account** (username +
password, 6+ characters). This account holds your boat's data and lets it sync
across your devices.

**Save your recovery code.** Right after signup the app shows a one-time recovery
code. Write it down and store it somewhere safe that isn't only on the phone.
It's the only way to regain access if you forget your password.

> ⚓ Create your account and set up every device **while you have wifi or cell
> signal.** Offshore the app works fully, but you can't create an account or add
> a brand-new device without connectivity.

### Logging in
After setup, opening the app asks for your password. If you enabled Face ID /
fingerprint (see below), tap **Unlock with Face ID** instead. Your password is
always available as a fallback.

### Face ID / fingerprint unlock
Go to **⚙️ Settings → Face ID / fingerprint unlock → Turn on** and approve the
prompt. From then on you can unlock with a glance. Your data stays encrypted —
your face/finger simply releases the key.

## The five sections

### 🧭 Ship's Stores (Inventory)
Track everything aboard, organized by compartment/locker. Add items with
quantities and units, adjust as you use them, and label lockers. Use it to know
what you have and where it lives.

### ⛵ Voyage Log
- **Start a voyage**, give it a name/destination.
- **Log entries** capture position and conditions. Tap **📍** to fill your
  current GPS latitude/longitude automatically, or type them by hand.
- **Date & time** default to now; tap **Edit** to backdate an entry you forgot.
- Record COG, SOG, AWA, AWS and notes.
- **Export a voyage to PDF** for your records.
- Finish a voyage when done; reopen past voyages anytime.

### 🔧 Maintenance
Log work done (who, when, notes) and plan **future projects** with parts lists.
A running history of everything you've serviced.

### 🛒 Provisions
Build and manage provisioning lists by category, check off what you have, and
generate shopping lists. Move provisions into stores/lockers when you stow them.

### 🆘 Ditch Bag (Abandon Ship)
Your emergency checklist and standard operating procedure. Keep the ditch-bag
contents and abandon-ship steps current and packed.

## Offline & syncing
- **Everything works offline.** Log, edit, and browse with no signal.
- When you're back in range, changes **sync automatically** to your other
  devices and to the cloud.
- All devices logged into the same boat share the same data.

## Backups
Open **⚙️ Settings**:
- **Download Full Backup (JSON)** — a complete backup file you can keep and
  re-import later. Do this before big trips.
- **Download Excel Backup** — a readable spreadsheet of your data.
- **Cloud Backups** — automatic timestamped snapshots you can restore from.

## Security
- Your data is **encrypted on each device** with your password, so a lost or
  stolen phone doesn't expose it.
- In the cloud, your data is protected and can't be listed/discovered by others.
- Keep your **recovery code** somewhere safe and separate from the phone.

## Troubleshooting

**Forgot password** — Login screen → **Forgot password?** → enter username +
recovery code → set a new password.

**App didn't update** — Reload normally (pull to refresh / reopen). Never use
"Clear Website Data" — it erases local data.

**Other device not syncing** — Ensure both have signal and reload each. Entries
sync within seconds when online.

**Need to add a device** — Do it with signal: open the app on the new device and
log in.

For anything else, see the FAQ or contact support.
