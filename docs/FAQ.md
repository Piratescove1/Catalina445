# Catalina 445 App — FAQ & Troubleshooting

### I forgot my password. How do I get back in?
On the login screen tap **Forgot password?**, enter your **username** and the
**recovery code** you saved when you created your account, then set a new
password. If you've lost the recovery code *and* the password, and no other
device is still logged in, that account's data can't be unlocked — this is why
saving the recovery code matters.

### Does it work with no internet / offshore?
Yes. Once your account is set up (done with signal), the app runs fully offline —
logging, inventory, everything. Changes sync automatically when you're back in
wifi/cell range. The one thing that needs signal is **creating an account or
setting up a brand-new device**.

### My other device isn't showing new changes.
Make sure both devices have signal and reload each one. New entries sync within a
few seconds when online. If it persists, note it — cross-device syncing has an
active improvement in progress (shared keys).

### How do I add another phone or tablet?
While you have signal, open the app on the new device and log in. Devices on the
same boat share the same data.

### Is my data safe if I lose my phone?
Yes. Your data is **encrypted on the device** with your password — someone who
finds the phone can't read it without your password (or Face ID). Keep the
recovery code somewhere separate.

### How do I back up my data?
**⚙️ gear → Download Full Backup (JSON)** saves everything to a file you can keep
or re-import later. There's also **Download Excel Backup** for a readable
spreadsheet, and **Cloud Backups** which keeps automatic timestamped snapshots
you can restore.

### The app didn't update after a new version shipped.
Just reload the page normally (pull down to refresh, or close and reopen). Do
**not** use Safari's "Clear Website Data" — that erases your local data.

### How do I enter GPS in the voyage log?
Tap the 📍 button to fill your current position automatically, or type latitude
and longitude by hand. You can also tap **Edit** on the date/time to backdate an
entry (e.g. a log you forgot to record).

### Can the crew each have their own login?
That's the plan — individual logins that all share the one boat. The shared-key
handoff that makes this seamless across devices is in progress.
